<p>Zapl Lab is an Android client for the <a href="https://github.com/XTLS/Xray-core">Xray core</a>. It can turn the VPN on and off automatically depending on the app you are using.</p>

<p>Zapl Lab is an independent modified distribution of the open-source <a href="https://github.com/2dust/v2rayNG">v2rayNG</a> project. It is not an official v2rayNG release and is not endorsed by the v2rayNG authors.</p>

<h3>Features</h3>

<ul>
<li>Automatic VPN control: choose which apps go through the VPN, which do not, and which always turn the VPN off.</li>
<li>VPN, proxy-only and root run modes, per-app proxy, routing rules and subscriptions inherited from v2rayNG.</li>
</ul>
